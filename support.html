Troubleshooting Common Issues

  • App Crashing: Ensure you have the latest update installed from the App Store. Try restarting your device.
  • Exporting fails: Check if you have granted the app permission to access your photo library or local storage in your device settings.
  • Purchase Restore: If your premium features aren't showing up, use the "Restore Purchases" button inside the app settings.
